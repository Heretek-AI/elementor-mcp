# Dental Clinic Industry Pack

## Conversion Architecture
- **Primary CTA**: "Book Appointment" / "Emergency Dental Care" in header and sticky hero.
- **Trust Elements**: Dentist credentials, before/after smile gallery, patient reviews, accepted insurance badges.
- **Core Sections**:
  1. Hero with immediate booking modal/form.
  2. Treatments grid (Cleanings, Whitening, Invisalign, Implants).
  3. Patient comfort & technology showcase.
  4. Dentist bio & warm team photography.
  5. Interactive map & clinic hours.
