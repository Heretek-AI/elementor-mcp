# Medical & Healthcare Industry Pack

## Conversion Architecture
- **Primary CTA**: "Find a Doctor" / "Patient Portal Login" / "Schedule Visit".
- **Trust Elements**: Board-certified physician badges, hospital affiliations, HIPAA compliance notice.
- **Core Sections**:
  1. Reassuring hero with specialty search bar.
  2. Clinical specialties & outpatient department grid.
  3. Physician directory with filters for language, specialty, and gender.
  4. New patient intake information & accepted insurance plans.
  5. Telehealth appointment scheduling instructions.
