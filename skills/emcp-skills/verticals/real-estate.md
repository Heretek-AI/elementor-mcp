# Real Estate Industry Pack

## Conversion Architecture
- **Primary CTA**: "Browse Properties" / "Request Free Home Valuation".
- **Trust Elements**: Recent sales volume, MLS integration, local neighborhood expertise, client reviews.
- **Core Sections**:
  1. Searchable hero with location, price, and property type filters.
  2. Featured listings grid with photo carousels and price tags.
  3. Neighborhood guides and school district ratings.
  4. Seller resources & instant valuation calculator.
  5. Agent bio and direct WhatsApp/call buttons.
