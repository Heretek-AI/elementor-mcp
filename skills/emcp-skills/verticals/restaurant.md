# Restaurant & Cafe Industry Pack

## Conversion Architecture
- **Primary CTA**: "Reserve a Table" / "Order Online" / "View Seasonal Menu".
- **Trust Elements**: Chef accolades, dietary icons (GF, Vegan, Halal), hygiene ratings, press mentions.
- **Core Sections**:
  1. Full-bleed atmospheric hero with reservation bar.
  2. Interactive food & beverage menu with pricing and allergens.
  3. Ambiance & culinary gallery.
  4. Private dining & catering inquiries.
  5. Hours, location, parking, and reservation widget (OpenTable/Resy).
